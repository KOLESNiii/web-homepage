`reverie.mp3` is adapted from the author's MP3: trimmed to original seconds
1.4–221.9, normalized to -20 LUFS / -3 dBTP, given 40 ms / 100 ms edge fades,
and re-encoded as 44.1 kHz stereo MP3 at 128 kbps. The app crossfades repeats.
No endorsement by the artist is implied.

Reproduce with FFmpeg (not required for normal setup or builds):

```sh
ffmpeg -i sb_reverie.mp3 \
  -af 'atrim=start=1.4:end=221.9,asetpts=PTS-STARTPTS,loudnorm=I=-20:TP=-3:LRA=11,afade=t=in:d=0.04,afade=t=out:st=220.4:d=0.1' \
  -ar 44100 -b:a 128k -map_metadata 0 reverie.mp3
```
